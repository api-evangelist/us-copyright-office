SA1-2 Short Form Statement of Account forms for Secondary Transmissions by Cable Systems mailed to cable operators on or about December 10, 2007, have improperly encoded barcodes printed on them in Space B on the form's front page. To rectify this, the Copyright Office mailed new barcode labels to each cable system operator on December 24, 2007. <br> <br>(1/24/08)
